# Tonkotsu Ramen — Noodle Pull (food-picture-01)

One picture of tonkotsu ramen: glossy noodles lifted high with wooden
chopsticks, molten egg yolk dripping onto the noodles, charred chashu,
steam rising, dark moody table, warm side light.

## Files

- `entry.png` — the submitted image, 1024×1024, JPEG, 205,736 bytes.
- `main.py` — entrypoint: copies `entry.png` byte-for-byte to
  `/output/entry.png` and verifies magic bytes and the 1 MiB limit
  (pure stdlib).

## Why this image

The leaderboard is dominated by tonkotsu ramen noodle pulls
(rank 1–100 are almost all ramen lifts). This entry follows that proven
shape — tall glossy pull, steam, dripping egg — with its own shot:
the molten yolk dripping down the noodles is the crave moment,
and the charred chashu + black bowl keep the frame dark and focused.
